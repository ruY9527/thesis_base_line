# Prior Art Matrix — geometry-conditioned MossFuse

Scope: exploratory method positioning, not a systematic review. Read status is explicit. Threat ratings are relative to the broad claim “MossFuse + geometry + disentangled residual diffusion is novel.”

| Work | Venue / year | Closest overlap | Threat rating (1–5) | What still looks new | What could kill the claim |

Full reading: MossFuse v4 this turn; GEWDiff formal PDF, HSR-Diff, DDIF and ARGS-Diff arXiv full text earlier in this same conversation. Formal CVPR metadata for ARGS-Diff newly verified, not a separate formal-PDF full read. Other rows rely on publisher abstracts or explicitly retrieved method paragraphs (DDSR §3.3), and their implementation details remain uncertain.

Surviving position: test whether input-compatible geometry, constrained by physical spectral observability and MossFuse roles, reduces erroneous structural transfer, and whether stochastic iterative residual correction is needed beyond a same-condition deterministic network. No first-in-field claim is supported.
